Overview
########

The heart of the LimeSDR Mini board is the Lattice ECP5 (`LFE5U-45F`_) FPGA. Its main function is to transfer digital data between the LMS7002M RF transceiver and a PC through a USB 3.0 connector. The block diagram of the LimeSDR Mini board is presented in Figure 3.

.. figure:: /images/LimeSDR-Mini_v2.4_diagrams_BD.png
  :width: 600

  Figure 3. LimeSDR Mini v2.4 board block diagram

This section describes the locations of components on the board. LimeSDR Mini v2.4 board top and bottom views with highlighted connectors and main components are presented in Figure 4 and Figure 5, respectively.

.. figure:: /images/LimeSDR-Mini_v2.4_components_top.png
  :width: 600

  Figure 4. LimeSDR Mini v2.4 board top connectors and main components

.. figure:: /images/LimeSDR-Mini_v2.4_components_bot.png
  :width: 600

  Figure 5. LimeSDR Mini v2.4 board bottom connectors and main components
